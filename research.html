04 · Product

A commercial decision engine between raw tariffs and pricing decisions

An enterprise TMS extends into shipment execution, customs, accounting, warehousing, tracking and operational workflows. BestModal's public positioning is much more concentrated: ingest commercial rate information, interpret its rules, calculate the complete transport cost, compare alternatives, and expose that price foundation to procurement, sales, RFQ/tender and potentially external marketplace users. It converts fragmented carrier and supplier tariffs, local charges, surcharges, transport legs and customer-specific rules into a comparable all-in transport price — one calculation environment spanning FCL, LCL, FTL, LTL, rail, barge and air, including BAF, CAF, PSS, THC, tolls, terminal costs, low-water surcharges, minimum charges, NAC/FAK/special rates, currencies and customer-specific logic.

06 · Product · Diligence flag

Air freight: website and management do not yet say the same thing

Website (Aug 2026)

Describes air freight as part of the designed/supported modal set — air rate structures, fuel/security charges and add-ons appear in the target feature set.

Management input

States that air remains the final item on the roadmap — not yet at the same production maturity as the other modes.

For investor materials, these must not be conflated. The safest positioning today:

BestModal's architecture and public product proposition span all major forwarding modes, but management indicates that air freight has not yet reached the same production maturity as the other modes.

The distinction matters because air-rate management is already the most digitised slice of the space: cargo.one has strong air foundations and has now added ocean through Cargofive, while Freightos/WebCargo operates a substantial air-pricing and booking network. Completing air is therefore important not merely to tick a modal box, but to remove an obvious competitive objection in global-forwarder sales — and it should be resolved before any deck describes BestModal as fully all-mode in production.

07 · Product · Core differentiation

Full-chain cost construction, not isolated rate storage

BestModal explicitly models pre-carriage, main carriage and on-carriage — including local charges at origin and destination — and combines the legs into one valid multimodal price. That matters because a "Hamburg→Shanghai ocean rate" and the "actual door-to-door cost a forwarder can quote a customer" are not equivalent commercial objects.

Full-chain construction is the report's assessment of BestModal's strongest conceptual differentiation vs. storing rate sheets.

Tariff owners can selectively release rates to chosen counterparties.

If enough suppliers and buyers use the same tariff substrate, this could eventually create data / network effects. But there is not enough public evidence yet to regard that network effect as established.

Value the company principally as workflow / rate-management SaaS today — and treat marketplace effects as upside, not thesis.
09 · Product · Operational watch item

SaaS front end — but how much tariff labour behind it?

BestModal states that tariff maintenance can be customer-operated, imported, or performed jointly with BestModal. A product that looks like SaaS on the front end but requires intensive ongoing tariff operations behind the scenes has very different economics from a genuinely automated software platform. Automation levels and delivery gross margin are therefore first-order diligence items.

"One system for multiple modes" is not the moat

Already claimed by others

  • CargoWise markets multimodal rate comparison
  • Transporeon calculates across road, ocean, air & other modes — and explicitly compares ocean vs rail
  • Freightos markets multimodal rate management
  • Cargobase covers air / ocean / rail / road

Where BestModal's public material is rarer

  • Barge tariff calculation made explicit
  • Low-water surcharges handled natively
  • Full inland-terminal combination logic
  • Complete pre-/main-/on-carriage in one engine
  • TMS-neutrality: coexist with CargoWise, legacy ERPs and local forwarding systems rather than replacing them — Magaya's own API-agnostic pitch proves forwarders value rate tech that avoids a back-office migration

Public rarity does not prove technical superiority — it identifies the territory in which superiority must be demonstrated. Diligence should test whether TMS coexistence actually occurs in customers today.

26 · Commercial model

Country × direction: the unit that builds the model

S = monthly platform subscription for one country-direction unit — one country, one direction (import or export). Contracts run for at least 12 months.

Customer ARR = 12 × S × country-direction units
Market ARR capacity = 12 × S × addressable units

Worked example (per management's model): if Scan Global Logistics used BestModal for export in five countries and import in two, it holds seven billable units → MRR = 7 × S, ACV = 84 × S. A larger organisation running both directions in ten countries holds 20 units → 240 × S = €240,000/yr at the €1,000 sensitivity.

28 · Commercial model · Issues to test

Where the country model needs stress-testing

Value mapping

A German country-direction subscription may serve a €1bn national operation, while another country subscription supports a tiny branch. A flat country multiplier does not always map perfectly to economic value.

Perceived double charging

Import and export as separate chargeable units is logically defensible — tariff structures and teams can differ — but procurement teams may see double charging when both directions use the same foundation. Packaging and enterprise caps could become relevant in very large roll-outs.

Different value metrics in competition

CargoWise's current Value Pack model charges transaction fees on eligible logistics transactions, moving away from seats/hosting/modules. BestModal's country pricing creates a very different purchase comparison: predictability and unlimited internal usage vs. costs linked to operational transaction volumes.

Service intensity (the big one)

Tariff data can be jointly managed with BestModal's team. If a €50,000 ARR contract requires frequent manual rate-sheet interpretation by BestModal personnel, the company should not be valued on software revenue alone without examining the associated delivery costs.

  • Descartes acquired Portrix specifically for multimodal rate management (~€20.7m, 2021)
  • Freightos acquired Shipsta for freight procurement (Aug 2024 → Freightos Procure)
  • cargo.one bought Cargofive to extend its rate-data infrastructure (Feb 2026, + ~$20m investment)
  • WiseTech continues expanding through major acquisitions (e2open, $2.1bn EV, 2025)
  • Trimble paid €1.88bn for the broader Transporeon transportation-software platform (2022)
The acquisition history suggests that strategically valuable freight-software assets are regularly consolidated into larger logistics-technology suites.
32 · Investment thesis

The strongest form of the thesis

BestModal first becomes the best independent pricing engine for complex European multimodal forwarding; then uses that calculation engine to expand geographically, add air, increase carrier/data connectivity — and establish a standard pricing layer between carrier tariffs and forwarder TMS systems.

The most credible investor framing — stronger than "multimodal logistics platform", which many competitors can claim:

A specialised freight-pricing infrastructure company beginning in European forwarding, using a common tariff and calculation engine to solve all-in, multimodal pricing across fragmented modes and national organisations, with a country-based land-and-expand business model.

Why Germany is the right beachhead

Large, cross-modal home market

The local forwarding industry is large and cross-modal, containing globally active forwarders as well as sophisticated owner-managed companies.

Product-market terrain

Rail, inland-waterway and North-European port/hinterland pricing — the area where BestModal's public capability appears more detailed than many ocean/air-first competitors.

Nested expansion path

Europe offers an order-of-magnitude larger account pool; the global forwarding market approximately doubles the European represented universe.
